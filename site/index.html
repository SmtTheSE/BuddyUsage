Know where you stand with every AI assistant you use

A limit should never be a surprise. BuddyUsage is a small island on the edge of your screen that shows how much of your Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Cursor and Copilot plan you have used, and when each limit resets. It also watches the coding agents running on your machine: stop one, nudge one, get told when one needs you, and guard your limits, from your desk or your phone.

Install

Pick your computer. Every download is the latest release from GitHub. After that, BuddyUsage updates itself.

Download for Apple Silicon Download for Intel

Not sure which? Apple menu, then About This Mac. "Apple M" means Apple Silicon.

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag BuddyUsage onto Applications.
  2. Open Applications and double-click BuddyUsage. The island appears on the right edge; its icon sits in the menu bar.
  3. Click a ring, then Sign in. A normal browser window opens on that provider's own login page. Once per provider.
If macOS says it "could not verify" the app Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway, then confirm. This happens once. BuddyUsage is open source and not yet notarized with a paid Apple Developer ID. On older macOS, right-click the app and choose Open.
Install from Terminal instead (no security prompt) 
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/SmtTheSE/BuddyUsage/main/install.sh | bash

Homebrew:

brew tap SmtTheSE/buddyusage https://github.com/SmtTheSE/BuddyUsage
brew trust SmtTheSE/buddyusage
brew install --cask buddyusage
Download for Windows Windows on ARM
  1. Run the downloaded installer. It installs for your user only, with no admin prompt, and starts automatically.
  2. The island appears on the right edge; the icon sits in the system tray, next to the clock. If it is hidden behind the ^ arrow, drag it onto the taskbar.
  3. Click a ring, then Sign in. Once per provider.
If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC" Click More info, then Run anyway. SmartScreen is noting that the installer is not code-signed. It happens once.
PowerShell one-liner 
irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/SmtTheSE/BuddyUsage/main/install.ps1 | iex
Download AppImage (x86_64) AppImage (ARM64)
  1. Make it executable: right-click, Properties, "Allow executing as program" (or chmod +x).
  2. Double-click to run. The island docks to the right edge of your primary display.
  3. Click a ring, then Sign in. Once per provider.
Good to know Transparency needs a compositing window manager. Click-through on the island's empty margin is not available on Linux.
Terminal one-liner 
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/SmtTheSE/BuddyUsage/main/install.sh | bash
